IAEA infrastructure review (INIR Phase 1)

IAEA mission to Kigali, 2–9 March 2026 · as reported from the IAEA press release

Team
Experts from Egypt, Estonia, Kenya and Pakistan, and six IAEA staff members
Scope
All 19 infrastructure issues of the IAEA Milestones Approach, Phase 1
Progress noted
Drafting a comprehensive nuclear law, enhancing the regulatory framework, conducting site surveys and identifying candidate locations
Further work
Finalize reports to support national decision-making, complete legislative reviews, and refine policies and strategies for nuclear power deployment
Next step
The IAEA and Rwanda will develop a coordinated workplan to continue supporting the programme

Sources: Capital FM · NucNet

RAEB projects

The three flagship projects listed by the Rwanda Atomic Energy Board.

Power

Small Modular Reactor

Nuclear power plant based on SMR technology to meet growing electricity demand. First unit targeted for the early 2030s; 55 MW in 2033 in the least-cost plan's strategic case. An SMR roadmap was signed with Rosatom at the first Joint Coordinating Committee meeting in 2026.

raeb.gov.rw/projects · LCPDP 2024 Review

Research & training

Centre for Nuclear Science and Technology

Research reactor and laboratories, established under an intergovernmental agreement with the Russian Federation (October 2019). Planned as a hub for research, workforce training and medical isotope production. The IAEA reviewed Rwanda's readiness for it in an INIR mission for research reactors, 13–21 July 2026.

raeb.gov.rw/projects · RAEB news, 24 July 2026

Advanced reactors

Critical Demonstration Experiment

Cooperation agreement signed in September 2023 with Dual Fluid Energy Inc., a Canadian-German nuclear technology company, for a demonstration experiment in Rwanda.
